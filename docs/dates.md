## Set the dates

Each record has two separate dates: **Next review** and **Expiry**. Set them when
you upload, or later with **Edit filing and responsibility**.

## See what is due

The library's **Review and expiry work** panel lists every scheduled date on the
records you can access:

- **Due**: the date is today or has passed.
- **Upcoming**: the date is still ahead.
- **Completed with evidence**: someone recorded the work for that date.

With no dates set it shows **No scheduled work.**

## Record the work

On the record, under **Complete investigated work**, choose the **Work item**
(**Review date** or **Expiry date**), describe what you checked in
**Evidence of investigation**, and press **Record completion**. Chest keeps the
evidence with who recorded it and when. Completing work never deletes or
changes the record itself.

When the next review or a renewed expiry is due, set the new date on the record;
it appears on the list again.
