Every file uploaded to Chest is kept exactly as it arrived. A replacement adds a
new version; it never overwrites the old one.

## Submit a replacement

On the record, press **Submit replacement**, choose the **Replacement file** and
press **Submit new immutable version**. The new version appears at the top of
**Versions and accountable decisions** as *submitted*, with its size, type,
SHA-256 fingerprint, who uploaded it and who is assigned to review it.

The **Current approved version** does not change. Everyone who opens the record
still sees and downloads the approved one until the new version is approved.

## Record a decision

The assigned reviewer chooses a **Decision** on the version and presses
**Record action**, with an optional **Comment / reason**:

- **Comment**: adds a note to that version.
- **Request changes**: marks that version as needing changes. To resubmit,
  upload a replacement; the request stays attached to the earlier version.
- **Approve this exact version**: makes this version the current approved one.
- **Assign reviewer**: hands the review to the person chosen in
  **Reviewer (for assignment)**.

Approval binds to the exact version the reviewer chose. Approving one version
never approves a later one that arrived meanwhile; each needs its own approval.
Only the assigned reviewer, with current reviewer access, can approve.

## Read the history

Each version lists its decisions in order: what was done, by whom and when, with
any comment. The approved version carries the **Current approved** tab. Earlier
versions and their decisions stay on the record for good.
