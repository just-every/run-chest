## Reversible trash

Only the owner can move a record to trash: **Move to reversible trash** at the
foot of the record. A trashed record leaves the library for everyone else, but
nothing is destroyed: every original, version and decision is kept.

The owner sees trashed records through **Open reversible trash** in the library,
and brings one back with **Restore from trash** on the record. **Active records**
returns to the library. Chest never empties trash on its own.

## The audit trail

Chest records every change it makes: uploads, filing changes, decisions, grants,
completions, trash and restores, each with who made it and when. **Audit** in the
top bar shows the latest 300 entries. The owner sees every entry; other people
see the entries for records they can access.
